Method & caveats

Token counts are real (Hermes state.db). $ = modelled from DeepSeek's official peak/off-peak rates (api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing), applied at each session's actual UTC timestamp. deepseek-chat is billed at Flash rates (legacy name routes to the Flash line). Cache-read tokens billed at the cache-hit rate.
Projection is a straight run-rate, not a forecast of the work — a large interactive day lifts it.
Interactive (Telegram) sessions re-send the full conversation each turn, so they dominate input+cache tokens: this is the single biggest lever on spend.
Cost rises with task size and context length, not with the number of jobs per se.
